Privacy Policy: Piquiyo LinkedIn Radar

Last updated: [CONFIRM: publish date]

Piquiyo LinkedIn Radar is a LinkedIn analytics dashboard run by Piquiyo (Ismael Cruces, Málaga, Spain), available at backoffice.piquiyo.com. This page explains what we collect when you use it, including the free plan, why, where it is kept, for how long, and how to delete it. The separate Piquiyo LinkedIn Capture browser extension has its own policy.

Who is responsible

Piquiyo, Ismael Cruces, Málaga, Spain, is the data controller. Contact: ismaelcruc@gmail.com.

Signing in with Google

You sign in with your Google account. Google tells us your name and email address and confirms that you own that email. We never see your Google password and we get no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Creating a free account stores your name, email, the date you joined and your plan.

What LinkedIn data we store

On the free plan you give us up to four LinkedIn links: your own profile, your company page, and two competitors (people or company pages). For those profiles we store publicly visible information only:

  • the profile or page name, link and photo or logo, and its follower count;
  • its public posts: text, date, format, image, and the public counts of reactions, comments and reposts;
  • a dated history of those counts, so the dashboard can show trends.

This information is collected from public LinkedIn pages by a data provider (Apify) on our behalf, about once a week. We never use your LinkedIn login, never post or message on your behalf, and never read private messages, connections or analytics that only you can see.

Competitors and other people. When you add a competitor, we store the same public information about them. We do this to give you a benchmark, which we consider a legitimate interest, and we keep it limited to what the page shows publicly. Anyone whose profile is tracked can ask us to stop and delete it by writing to the contact address above.

Other data

  • Activity log: sign-ins and changes you make (for example, adding a profile), kept for security and to fix problems.
  • Product analytics: which pages are opened, recorded on our server with PostHog (EU servers), without advertising cookies. [CONFIRM: only if the PostHog key is enabled]
  • Emails: if we email you, delivery goes through our email provider. [CONFIRM: Resend or Brevo]

How long we keep it

  • Your account and the data of the profiles you track: for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, everything listed above is removed immediately from the live database: your account, your workspace, the profiles you added, their posts and history, and your entries in the activity log. A short note that "an account was deleted" is kept, without your email.
  • Database backups roll over automatically and are gone within [CONFIRM: Neon history retention, e.g. 7 days].

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open the sidebar and choose Delete my account, then confirm. This deletes everything at once and signs you out. You can also email ismaelcruc@gmail.com and we will do it for you within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Write to the contact address above. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD).

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
